System Architecture Slide 4 / 10
Solving The Twist
Mastering Low-Light & Grainy Dashcam Footage
⚡ THE TWIST: Mysuru doesn't have smart cameras watching every street at night. The system must succeed on realistic, grainy, moving dashcam feeds in pitch-black conditions.
CLAHE Histogram Equalization
Applies tile-based adaptive contrast. Brightens shadow regions while preventing oncoming vehicle headlight glare from blowing out the frame.
Non-Linear Gamma Scaling (γ=0.7)
Reveals concrete rubble, laterite red bricks, and plastic waste against dark unpaved asphalt without amplifying background grain.
Weather & Rain Calibration
Monsoon Mode dynamically down-weights confidence thresholds when windshield rain streaks cause blur, avoiding weather-induced false alarms.
